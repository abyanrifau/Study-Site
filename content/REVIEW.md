### Figures in examples

- [ ] All numbers in worked examples, drills and diagrams are **illustrative**, chosen to make the
      arithmetic clear. They are not real market data. Any figure you quote in an exam should come
      from the source in the question paper.
- [ ] The company names in the written practice questions (Island Bakery, Kite Retail, Harbour
      Marine, Cove Products, Reef Trading, Delta Foods, Norland Insurance, Meridian Tools, Pacific
      Resorts) are all invented, as are their figures, exactly as an exam source would be.
- [ ] Country A, B, X, Y, Z and W in the WEC14 essays are invented economies with invented data,
      again as in a real source booklet. No real country's statistics are quoted anywhere.
- [ ] The Maldives examples describe real structural features of the economy (tourism dominance,
      near-total import dependence, reef sensitivity, dispersed population) and deliberately quote
      **no figures**. Worth checking with your teacher that this is the framing they use.

### Things I deliberately left out because I could not confirm them at IAL level

- [ ] **Accounting command words.** The WAC12 specification has no command word appendix, unlike
      Business and Economics. The command drill for WAC12 uses the words that actually appear on
      WAC12 papers (Prepare, Calculate, Explain, Evaluate, Discuss, State, Complete). Worth
      confirming with your teacher.
- [ ] **ARR conventions differ between the two subjects, on purpose.** WBS13 uses average annual
      profit over the **initial** investment, as the Business specification presents it. WAC12
      uses average annual profit over the **average** investment, as the Accounting specification
      presents it. Both are in the notes. Check your teacher uses the same split, because using
      the wrong one loses the mark.
- [ ] **Gearing** has more than one accepted formula. The notes use fixed-return capital over
      total long-term capital and tell you to state the version you have used. Confirm your
      teacher accepts that.
- [ ] **Quota diagram.** The WEC14 quota diagram reads the new price off the demand curve at the
      quota limit, which is the standard teaching simplification. A stricter treatment shifts the
      whole supply curve right by the quota quantity. If your teacher draws it the second way, say
      so and I will redraw it.
- [ ] **Prebisch-Singer** is presented as a hypothesis with its reasoning and its counter-evidence,
      not as settled fact, because the empirical evidence is genuinely contested.

### What this means for how the site is set up

- **WEC14 is 74 of its 80 marks in essays and data response**, which is why its written practice
  pack is the largest in the site: four full annotated essays, six calculation drills and 141
  evaluation points.
- **WAC12 is almost entirely calculation with written parts attached**, which is why its pack has
  ten worked drills, each with a different set of numbers to try afterwards.
- **WEC14 is the only unit with real multiple choice questions in the exam**, so its 325-question
  bank maps directly onto Section A as well as being a revision tool.

### Elasticity conventions used in the notes

Worth confirming your teacher uses the same conventions, as they vary between textbooks:

- **PED** is presented as normally negative, with the advice to compare its size to 1 while
  ignoring the sign.
- **YED** is presented with the sign retained, because positive/negative is what separates a
  normal good from an inferior one.
- **Percentage change** always divides by the original value. The midpoint or arc method is not
  used, since the specification does not require it.

## Rules being followed

- Notes and questions are written from scratch. Nothing is copied from textbooks, revision sites,
  past papers or the material in the `Files` folder. Several items in that folder are published
  revision guides, and they are also for **UK A Level Business (9BS0)** rather than IAL, so they
  were used only as background reading and as a check on what your teachers emphasise.
- No past paper question appears anywhere in the question banks or the written practice. The past
  papers in the library are the original Pearson PDFs, downloaded only from
  qualifications.pearson.com, and they are kept separate from the site's own content.
- Topic lists, topic order, assessment objectives, command words and exam structures come from the
  official specifications in `/specs` only.
- If a fact, figure or definition cannot be confirmed at IAL level, it is left out of the notes and
  listed here instead.
- British English spelling throughout.
