# Specifications needed here

The topic list, topic order, assessment objectives, exam structure and command words for every
unit come from these PDFs and nothing else. Until a unit's specification is in this folder, that
unit's topic list stays empty and its page says so.

**All six of your units are covered by three PDFs.** Save each one into this folder with the
filename shown.

| Save as | Covers | Download from |

| `IAL-Business-Spec.pdf` | WBS11, WBS12, WBS13, WBS14 | [International A Level Business specification (2018)](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20Advanced%20Level/Business/2018/Specification-and-Sample-Assessment/International-A-Level-Business-Spec.pdf) |

## A note on the `Files` folder

The material already in the project's `Files` folder is mostly **UK A Level Business (9BS0)**, not
International A Level. The two share a lot of content and the same theme numbering, but they are
different qualifications with different papers and different unit structures. Several of those
files are also published revision guides and third-party resources, which means they can be read
for background but must never be copied into this site.

So: the specifications above are the source of truth. The `Files` folder is background reading
only.
